Counted Out Hall of Fame

  • QB Doug Flutie

    Counted out: 5-foot-10, and Boston College was his only Division I scholarship offer.

    Proved ’em wrong: 1984 Heisman, a record six CFL Most Outstanding Player awards, then a Pro Bowl with the Bills at 35.

  • QB Vinny Testaverde

    Counted out: Threw 35 interceptions in his second NFL season.

    Proved ’em wrong: Led the 1998 Jets to the AFC Championship Game and played 21 NFL seasons.

  • QB Vince Young

    Counted out: Critics said his side-arm throwing motion would never work.

    Proved ’em wrong: 467 total yards to beat USC in the 2006 Rose Bowl, then NFL Offensive Rookie of the Year.

  • RB Darren Sproles

    Counted out: Too small to be an every-down NFL back, they said.

    Proved ’em wrong: Set the NFL single-season record with 2,696 all-purpose yards in 2011.

  • QB Cam Ward

    Counted out: Zero-star recruit whose only scholarship offer came from FCS Incarnate Word.

    Proved ’em wrong: No. 1 overall pick in the 2025 NFL Draft.
